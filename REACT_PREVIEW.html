📦 Finding Your SharePoint App Catalog

🎯 Where to Upload Your SPFx Package

The App Catalog is where you deploy your .sppkg file to make it available across SharePoint sites.

🔍 Most Common Location

Tenant App Catalog URL:
https://[yourtenant].sharepoint.com/sites/appcatalog

Example:

  • https://contoso.sharepoint.com/sites/appcatalog
  • https://fabrikam.sharepoint.com/sites/appcatalog

📋 How to Find Your Tenant Name

  1. Look at any SharePoint URL you have
  2. Find the part before .sharepoint.com
  3. That's your tenant name!

From URL: https://contoso.sharepoint.com/sites/marketing
Tenant name: contoso

⚠️ Can't Find App Catalog?

If you get "Site not found":

  • Ask your IT administrator for the App Catalog URL
  • Check SharePoint Admin Center (admin rights required)
  • Use site collection app catalog as alternative
